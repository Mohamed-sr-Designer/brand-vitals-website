01 · Approval Ledger

No more "I never approved that."

Clients comment on an exact spot of a design or an exact second of a video, then approve in one tap. Every approval is recorded with name, time and version.

  • Pinned comments on designs and video frames
  • Version history side by side
  • A ledger entry for every approval

02 · Scope Guard

Count revisions against what was agreed.

HQ tracks every revision round against the scope. When a request goes beyond it, the round is flagged and an extra invoice is one click away.

  • Deliverables and revision limits per client
  • Automatic out-of-scope flags
  • Extra invoice or waive, your call

03 · WhatsApp client loop

Clients reply where they already are.

Clients receive work and reply on WhatsApp. Their approval or feedback syncs back to the task and the ledger, so nothing lives only in a chat.

  • Send for review straight to WhatsApp
  • Replies synced to tasks and approvals
  • One inbox for every client chat

04 · Results next to the work

The ad they approved, with how it's performing.

Connect your ad accounts and the live performance of an approved ad appears on the same page as the approval. Clients see the work and the result together.

  • Meta and Google Ads results per approved asset
  • Shareable client report in one click
  • Spend, clicks and leads on one screen

05 · Built for this region

Arabic, Ramadan and EGP are not edge cases here.

A full right-to-left interface, a content calendar that knows Ramadan, Eid and national days, and invoices in EGP and SAR.

  • Arabic and English copy approved side by side
  • Regional calendar for Egypt and the GCC
  • Invoices in EGP and SAR with local gateways
